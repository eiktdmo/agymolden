⚡ Replacing Legacy Toolchains Across All Three Stages

Stage 1: Building & Inputs

Replacing Avogadro & Molden

Build, sketch, pre-optimize, and export production QM decks in seconds without syntax errors.

  • Multi-Engine Deck Generator (Ctrl+Shift+I): Gaussian, ORCA, Q-Chem, PySCF, openLOWDIN, newBRORSEN, xTB, and Multiwfn.
  • 2D Bond-Line Sketcher (Ctrl+Shift+K): ChemDraw-style sketching to clean 3D force-field coordinates.
  • Interactive 🧲 Tweezers: Pull and stretch any atom with live UFF physics relaxation without freezing.
  • Transition State Guess (IDPP / QST2): Collision-free reaction path interpolator with direct ORCA NEB-TS / Gaussian QST3 export.
  • 2D PES Grid Scan Builder: Automated 2D coordinate scan decks for ORCA and Gaussian ModRedundant.
Stage 2: Execution & Engines

Replacing Gabedit & Shell Scripts

Execute calculations locally or remotely on university HPC clusters without leaving the GUI.

  • In-App Universal Runner: Run ORCA, PySCF, and xTB directly with live log streaming.
  • Universal Foundation ML Potentials: Integrated MACE-MP-0 (89 elements) and GFN2-xTB for instant zero-cost geometries.
  • Pre-Flight Chemistry Checker: Auto-detects invalid spin multiplicities, radical valence errors, and unphysical steric clashes before launch.
  • HPC Cluster Queue Manager: Built-in Slurm / PBS job script generator and SSH file transfer.
  • QC Doctor: Scans failing logs and recommends exact convergence keywords (tight SCF, SOSCF, grid adjustments).
Stage 3: Advanced Analysis

Replacing GaussView & Chemcraft

Publication-grade workbenches for physical, organic, and quantum chemistry analysis.

  • Interactive Hückel Workbench (Ctrl+Shift+H): Live secular determinants, Frost circles, Aufbau spin arrows, and 3D LCAO lobes.
  • Eyring Reaction Kinetics & Tunneling: Free energy profiles (ΔG‡, ΔGrxn) with Wigner & Eckart quantum tunneling.
  • Marcus Photoredox Kinetics: Inner/outer-sphere reorganization (λ) and diabatic PES parabolas.
  • Catalysis Sterics: Buried Volume (%V_bur), SambVca-style 2D topographic maps, and Sterimol (L, B1, B5).
  • Open-Shell & Spin Diagnostics: <S²> contamination evaluation, Yamaguchi projection (E_AP), and Heisenberg J coupling.
  • Born-Haber Redox Workbench: Standard reduction potentials E° vs SHE / SCE / Fc/Fc⁺.
  • NBO E(2) Network & EDA: Natural Bond Orbital donor-acceptor chord graphs and SAPT energy decomposition.

🎯 Student Pre-Release Testing Assignments

Please test the three core stages on your laptop and report your observations:

Task 1 (Building & Inputs): Sketch or load a conjugated molecule (e.g. Butadiene or Pyridine via Ctrl+Shift+B). Use the 🧲 Tweezers tool to pull an atom. Verify that connected bonds stretch smoothly. Then open QM Calculation Input Generator (Ctrl+Shift+I) and export an ORCA or Gaussian input file.
Task 2 (Hückel Theory & Aromaticity): Open Tools → 🎓 Hückel Theory Workbench (Ctrl+Shift+H). Load Cyclobutadiene (antiaromatic 4π) vs. Benzene (aromatic 6π). Check the Frost circle, secular determinant, and project LCAO orbital lobes onto the 3D molecule.
Task 3 (Reaction Thermochemistry): Open Tools → ⚗️ Reaction Thermochemistry & Eyring Kinetics (Alt+Shift+R). Inspect activation free energies (ΔG‡), Eyring rate constants k(T), and quantum tunneling corrections.
