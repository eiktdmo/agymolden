🎓 Interactive Quantum Chemistry Course Modules

🎓 Hückel Molecular Orbital (HMO) Workbench

Shortcut: Ctrl + Shift + H (or Tools menu)

  • Secular Determinants: Live |H - xI| = 0 symbolic matrix display.
  • Frost-Musulin Circles: Inscribed polygon circle mnemonic for [N]annulenes.
  • Aufbau Spin Ladder: Interactive Hund's rule spin arrows (↑↓) across energy levels.
  • Coulson Bond Orders: Predicted bond orders (e.g., Benzene C-C = 0.667).
  • 3D LCAO Orbital Lobes: 1-click projection of MO lobes and phase signs to 3D atoms.
Class Exercise 1: Select the Cyclobutadiene preset. Notice the two degenerate SOMOs at α (antiaromatic 4π diradical). Now use the N_π spinner to add 2 electrons (Cyclobutadienyl dianion, 6π) and observe the aromatic stabilization!

🧲 Molecular Tweezers (Live Force Field Relaxation)

Shortcut: Click the 🧲 Tweezers ribbon button

  • Real-Time UFF Physics: Left-click and drag any atom in the 3D viewport.
  • Dynamic Topology: Connected bonds and angles stretch and relax in real time.
  • Intuitive Hand Cursors: Hover displays open hand; dragging shows grab cursor.
  • Fast Geometry Pre-optimization: Relax steric clashes in seconds before QM runs.
Class Exercise 2: Load Ethanol or Water. Activate Tweezers, grab a hydrogen or carbon atom, and pull it. Notice how the connected bond length and angles dynamically adapt.

⚡ Eyring Thermochemistry & Marcus Kinetics

Shortcut: Alt + Shift + R & Tools menu

  • Reaction Barriers (ΔG‡) & Rates: Eyring transition state theory k(T).
  • Quantum Tunneling: Wigner and Eckart transmission coefficient κ(T).
  • Marcus Electron Transfer: Inner/outer-sphere reorganization (λ_in, λ_out).
  • Normal vs. Inverted Regime: Live diabatic reactant/product PES parabolas.
Class Exercise 3: In Marcus Workbench, adjust ΔG° from -0.3 eV (Normal Activation) to -1.5 eV. Watch the product parabola shift into the famous Marcus Inverted Regime where reaction slows down!

⚛️ Open-Shell Spin Diagnostics & Redox Potentials

Shortcut: Available under the Tools menu

  • <S²> Contamination: Check deviation from ideal S(S+1).
  • Yamaguchi Projection: E_AP correction for broken-symmetry singlets.
  • Exchange Coupling (J): Antiferromagnetic (J < 0) vs. ferromagnetic (J > 0).
  • Born-Haber Redox Cycles: Standard reduction potentials E° vs SHE / SCE / Fc/Fc⁺.
Class Exercise 4: Inspect the Born-Haber cycle for a 1-electron reduction. See how differential continuum solvation (ΔΔG_solv) shifts the reduction potential by over 1.5 Volts.
